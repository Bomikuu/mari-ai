# Media workspace

Mode: Operate. A local project owner creates a short application video or auditions Mari's synthetic voice, then inspects and plays the actual saved artifact. This extends the established command center in place; it does not replace its visual world.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The delivered video or audio is the dominant object. A narrow preparation column and a wider playback area make the next action clear without adding another dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve Mari's flat light panels and midnight character palette, existing sans-serif hierarchy, restrained blue actions, gold focus and rose errors. Use existing shadcn buttons, fields and disclosure patterns.

STORY: Select a project, confirm its service is ready, set a brief and generate. Actual worker activity leads to a playable video. Voice remains explicitly started and manually played.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact title and back action precede one continuous panel divided into settings and output. Project identity and readiness appear above the brief. The primary generate action sits directly after its fields. Native playback, download and prior outputs occupy the wider right column.

FORM: Incumbent workspace extension; no random world selection. Signature interaction: selecting an earlier output replaces the player in place while preserving draft settings. No new decorative motion; inherited motion preferences remain intact.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

Delivered: `docs/design/media-finish-review.md` records **ship** after one initial Firefox batch and one factual-copy confirmation batch. Final captures/checks are in `.impeccable/review/media`; initial captures are archived in `round-1`. `docs/evidence/media/provenance.json` records the actual generated demo media and poster. No additional polish pass is required.
